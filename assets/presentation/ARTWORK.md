# Presentation illustration provenance

The two PNG assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool for presentation design. They are conceptual illustrations, not ACDC photographs, ground truth, measured predictions or evidence of model accuracy. Slide captions and presenter notes disclose this distinction. Actual measured prediction examples remain on the results slide.

- `cover_adverse_vru.png`: conceptual adverse-weather street panorama.
- `context_detail_concept.png`: conceptual illustration of broad context and detail at a larger scale. Colors are illustrative and do not encode actual model predictions.

## Cover prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a sophisticated editorial illustration for the COVER ART of an academic computer vision presentation about transformer segmentation of vulnerable road users in adverse weather. Wide panoramic aspect ratio 16:9, high resolution. A single continuous European urban street seen from a forward-facing vehicle, with one pedestrian and one cyclist visibly recognizable, transitioning across four atmospheric zones from left to right: diffuse fog, deep nighttime, rain with wet asphalt, and snow. A tasteful technical visualization: the pedestrian, rider and bicycle have sharply defined translucent coral, amber and turquoise semantic-segmentation regions, with the surrounding road and buildings subdued in deep midnight navy. Elegant cinematic photography-meets-scientific-illustration, restrained detail, a few precise thin patch-grid hints only near the road users, no sci-fi HUD, no dashboards, no icons. Strong silhouettes, dramatic lighting, sophisticated texture, minimal and legible from a distance. Keep all salient scene content in the RIGHT 60 percent and leave the LEFT 40 percent as very dark navy negative space, fading smoothly with no hard boundary, for editable white presentation title that will be added separately. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks. This is conceptual illustrative artwork, not an actual dataset image or measured prediction. Palette navy #132D46, aqua #66DFD2, coral #EF5E55, warm amber #F7B541. Make it visually compelling and polished for a master's research defense.

## Context/detail prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Create a clean, sophisticated concept illustration for a master's computer vision slide about MULTI-SCALE semantic segmentation of people and cyclists in adverse road scenes. Wide landscape 16:9. White background with generous whitespace. At LEFT show a small wide photographic-style illustrated European road scene at dusk with subtle fog, a pedestrian and cyclist, rendered mostly in cool slate navy. A thin amber crop rectangle highlights the cyclist. At RIGHT show an enlarged view of EXACTLY THE SAME highlighted cyclist and bicycle, with finer outline detail and amber/turquoise semantic color regions. A restrained very thin connector associates the crop to the detail, no arrows or flowchart boxes. Both views must clearly show one scene at different scales, not unrelated scenes. Do not depict a neural network, do not invent performance, no heatmaps or confidence, no numbers. Minimal editorial scientific art, precise clean edges, realistic proportions, navy #132D46, teal #007F86, amber #F7B541, coral #EF5E55. The main two images occupy the central 70% of the canvas with large margins, allowing editable labels to be added outside by the presentation author. NO TEXT, LETTERS, LABELS, logos or watermarks. This conceptual illustration explains broad context and high-resolution detail, not actual model output or ACDC data.
